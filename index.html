Frequently asked questions
Does Woodshed Pizza take reservations?

Woodshed Pizza does not take standard dining reservations. Contact the team for private party and event inquiries.

What kind of food do you serve?

Woodshed Pizza serves Neapolitan-style pizza, salads, appetizers and desserts in Point Pleasant, New Jersey.

Are vegan options available?

Yes, vegan pizza options are available. Please ask the team about current choices and ingredients.

Do you offer catering?

Yes. Woodshed offers off-premise catering. Email woodshedpizzanj@gmail.com to ask about your event.

Can I book a private party?

Yes, private party inquiries are welcome. Email woodshedpizzaevents@gmail.com or call 732-475-7218. Include “Woodshed Private Party” in the email subject.

What are your opening hours?

Wednesday through Saturday, 4 PM–9 PM, and Sunday, 4 PM–8 PM. The restaurant is closed Monday and Tuesday. Please check directly for holiday updates.
